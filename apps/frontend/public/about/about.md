# About HangBuddies

HangBuddies is an open-source web app that lets you create and share polls and date surveys in seconds—no registration or hassle required.

## What makes HangBuddies special?

- Create polls and surveys in seconds
- Share the link directly with friends, teams, or groups
- No sign-up or account needed
- Intuitive, modern, and responsive user interface
- Perfect for scheduling, quick votes, and group decisions
- Open source—everyone can contribute!

## Who is HangBuddies for?

Anyone who wants to quickly and easily poll a group or find a date—whether for private events, clubs, teams, or any occasion.

## Contribute & Feedback

HangBuddies thrives on community! Find the source code and more info on [GitHub](https://github.com/danieljanca/hangbuddies).

Feedback, ideas, and contributions are always welcome.
